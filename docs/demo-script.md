# BloomKnights judge-facing demo script

The canonical operator procedure is [`demo-day/runbook.md`](demo-day/runbook.md). This page is the compact spoken version.

## Hook

> “Prediction markets know their contracts, but they do not know what I am looking at. BloomKnights starts with the physical world. I look at a broadcast and it identifies the event, retrieves the relevant intelligence, and shows the model probability beside the market probability.”

## World Cup

Start the camera feed on the Argentina–France World Cup Final and press **Analyze**.

> “There is no sport picker. Cerebras recognized soccer, the World Cup Final, the participants, score, phase, and clock. BloomKnights then attached the matching precollected evidence pack: official timeline context, the sport-specific state model, and a clearly labeled replay-market reconstruction.”

Point to the evidence checks, then Model / Market / Gap.

> “The market number is mocked and labeled because this is a historical replay. What is real is the complete product loop and the automatic match between what the glasses see and the correct analytical context.”

## NBA Finals switch

Switch the source screen to Cavaliers–Warriors Game 7 without touching BloomKnights.

> “I only changed what I am watching. The prior soccer context is discarded, basketball is detected, and the NBA Finals timeline loads automatically.”

Point to **What changed** and **Next trigger**.

## Super Bowl swing

Show Super Bowl LI at 28–3 and then 28–28.

> “At 28–3, New England is a three-percent comeback tail. At 28–28, the same event has become essentially a coin flip. The probability moved because the visible score and clock moved—not because someone searched for another market.”

## UFC abstraction

Switch to UFC 229.

> “Now the state representation changes. There is no team score, so BloomKnights uses fighter identity, round, clock, and only visibly supported control context. It does not invent judge scores or damage.”

## Close

> “Four broadcasts. No picker. No search. One interface from visible reality to a market question. With funding, the precollected replay layer becomes live data and market infrastructure. Look at the game. See the probability.”

## Language rules

- Say “model estimate,” “market-implied probability,” and “gap.”
- Never say “guaranteed edge,” “risk-free,” or “the market is wrong.”
- Always acknowledge `Historical replay`, `Sport template`, and `MOCK` labels.
- If asked what is precollected: event evidence and replay-market checkpoints are precollected; camera ingestion, automatic visual identification, event switching, pack matching, and presentation are running live.
