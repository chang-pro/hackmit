# ReLoop — One-Liner Bank

Language rules (README §6) apply to every line here: model estimate vs. market-implied probability, never "guaranteed edge" / "beat the market" / "risk-free." Historical demos are labeled replays.

1. **Look at the game. See the probability.** *(the tagline — lead with this everywhere)*
2. Your glasses read the scoreboard. A model estimates the winner. The market gets compared. You never look away.
3. The broadcast is the input.
4. Prediction markets know their contracts. They don't know what you're looking at. ReLoop connects the two.
5. "Boston 68%. Market 59%." Whispered in your ear, straight from the scoreboard.
6. Smart glasses that turn a live game into a live probability, compared against Polymarket in real time.
7. Watch the model estimate and the market price fight each other during the 28–3 comeback.
8. From pixels to probability in one glance.
9. Five sports, one pipeline: NBA, NFL, soccer, UFC, golf.
10. The screen you're already watching becomes the query.
11. No app-switching, no contract-searching. Look, listen, understand.
12. Computer vision reads the game state. Deterministic math does the rest.
13. A wearable second opinion on every live market.
14. It refuses to answer when the scoreboard is blurry. That's the point.
15. Where what you see meets what the market believes.
