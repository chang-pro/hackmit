Super Bowl LI · February 2017

The market said 96%.

Atlanta led 28–3 in the third quarter. Live markets priced the Falcons near 96% to win. Then Tom Brady authored the largest comeback in Super Bowl history, and that price collapsed to zero in about 20 game minutes.

This is the moment BloomKnights is built for. When the game flips faster than the market breathes, the gap between what a model estimates and what the market implies is exactly the thing you want in your line of sight — live, at the moment it opens.

Built for Meta Ray-Ban

Your glasses see the game. Your phone keeps the receipts.

The companion iOS app pairs with your glasses, streams the readout, and keeps a session log of every state, estimate, and market snapshot — with timestamps and confidence, so you can audit what you saw.

  • Glance or listen. Read the HUD, or hear it: "Boston 68 percent. Market 59. Model is 9 points higher."
  • Confidence gating. If the scoreboard is unreadable or market data is stale, it says so instead of showing a stale number.
  • Privacy by default. Frames are processed for the analysis you asked for. Raw footage is not stored by default.
